# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite with static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No runtime dependencies; Vite is the only development dependency.

## Users

Families, friends, neighbors in Quilicura, and returning customers who discover Toffeelopi through Instagram and want to browse desserts and order by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

To present Toffeelopi's home-style dessert catalog, sizes, prices, availability, delivery or pickup information, and payment options, and help customers place an order through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Toffeelopi offers desserts for sharing at home in Quilicura. The cheesecake follows a family recipe; the pie and apple tart are offered on announced dates, and the vanilla cake is available anytime.

## Operating Context

The business is based in Quilicura, Santiago, Chile. Visitors commonly arrive from the Instagram bio link and use mobile devices. Orders require at least one day's notice. The pickup address is shared privately on WhatsApp. Delivery is within Quilicura, with the charge confirmed according to distance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The catalog includes cheesecake, lemon pie, vanilla cake, and apple tart.
- Cheesecake: regular (28 cm, CLP $18,500), small (20 cm, CLP $12,400), and a portion (CLP $2,000; do not state a portion size).
- Lemon pie: 28 cm, CLP $11,000.
- Vanilla cake: large, CLP $10,000; vanilla with orange zest and powdered sugar.
- Apple tart: 28 cm, CLP $10,000; with cream and apricot jam.
- Cheesecake sauce may be served separately or on the cheesecake.
- Cheesecake, lemon pie, and apple tart are offered on announced dates; the vanilla cake is available anytime. Do not invent dates or cup limits.
- Payment is by bank transfer when confirming the order, with the receipt sent by WhatsApp, or cash on receipt.
- The account holder approved listing the transfer details publicly on the page. The pickup address is still shared privately on WhatsApp.
- Effective and update dates for availability and prices remain undecided; hide them until supplied.
- Do not invent testimonials, reviews, sales figures, or availability.
- The project brief requires no runtime dependencies and Vite as the only development dependency.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand: Toffeelopi.
- Voice: warm, close, and natural Chilean Spanish without exaggeration.
- Confirmed palette: berry red `#9B1B30`, cream `#FDFBF7`, and toasted-gold `#C89B6B`.
- Instagram account: `@toffedulceria`.

## Product Principles

- Make each product, size, price, and current availability easy to understand.
- Make ordering through WhatsApp straightforward.
- Keep date-based availability and local delivery details accurate.
- Keep the pickup address private; show transfer details only as explicitly approved by the account holder.
- Never fill undecided business facts with invented values.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The website must be usable on mobile and desktop.
- Provide visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive image text, and touch targets of at least 44 px.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
